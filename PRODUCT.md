# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with TypeScript and handcrafted responsive CSS.

## Users

Primary users are retired and near-retirement engineering, technical and project-management professionals who want to keep contributing without returning to a conventional full-time role. They need dignified language, clear boundaries, readable interfaces and a low-friction way to express interest.

Secondary users are organizations with real engineering needs. The public homepage only needs to establish that the network serves real industrial work; it does not expose internal matching, pricing or delivery operations.

## Product Purpose

匠应达 is the public brand and portal for a future engineering-experience service platform. The current website explains the purpose of the platform, introduces service modes at a high level, establishes basic trust and attracts qualified senior professionals to apply.

The software platform and desktop client are not yet ready. “进入平台” and “下载桌面版” must not appear until those destinations exist and can be independently verified.

## Positioning

This is a focused product portal, not a retirement job board, open social community, public expert directory or encyclopedic explanation of the operating model. It should feel as calm and legible as a modern product company website while remaining warm and credible for older professionals.

## Current Public Scope

- Global navigation contains four destinations only: 首页 服务方式 项目保障 关于我们.
- Home: one clear promise, a full-bleed senior-professional scene, high-level previews of the three public content areas and one application action.
- Service modes: a dedicated page presenting three public categories only — professional consultation, project collaboration and experience transfer.
- Project assurance: a dedicated page presenting three principles only — mutual confirmation, clear boundaries and controlled information.
- About: a dedicated page explaining whom the network connects and using only the general phrase 高校 学院与行业组织.
- Join: a compact expression-of-interest form on the homepage. It does not appear in the global navigation. Submission is validated server-side and emailed to the approved contact mailbox when SMTP is configured; it does not create a platform account or write to the simulated MVP state.

## Current Exclusions

- No platform-entry or desktop-download action before the product is ready.
- No detailed matching logic, expert-passport fields, fee structure, contract clauses, insurance, tax arrangements or internal workflow.
- No fabricated expert, company, institution, partner, metric, testimonial or project case.
- No named relationship with 江西理工大学 or 原南方冶金学院 and no reference to a 校友圈试点.
- Do not add operating-entity or contact claims beyond the details explicitly approved for the privacy page and join-notification mailbox.

## Brand Commitments

- Public name: 匠应达.
- Brand descriptor: 工程专家经验服务平台.
- Primary lockup: the supplied transparent 匠应达 logo asset. The header uses the original palette; the dark footer uses an exact-geometry contrast variant that changes only the deep-teal pixels to the approved on-dark light teal while retaining the cyan and orange accents. Do not redraw or replace it with styled text.
- Voice: respectful, plain and confident; never pity-based, overtechnical or promotional for its own sake.
- Visual world: full-bleed human photography, bold editorial typography and interlocking cyan, orange, warm-white and deep-teal fields. Avoid the conventional left-copy/right-image enterprise template.
- Treat senior experts as active professionals whose judgment and accumulated methods still have practical value.
